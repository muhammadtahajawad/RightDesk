# RightDesk
### AI Legal Action Assistant for Pakistan

RightDesk converts real-life legal problems into actionable legal outputs.
Instead of explaining law, it gives you a ready FIR draft, your legal rights, and exact steps to take.

## Live App
[Click here to use RightDesk](https://huggingface.co/spaces/mtahajawad/RightDesk)

## What it does
- Understands your situation
- Explains your legal rights under Pakistani law
- Gives step-by-step immediate actions
- Generates a ready FIR or complaint document
- Tells you exactly where to escalate

## Built With
- Gradio
- Groq API (LLaMA 3.3 70B)
- Hugging Face Spaces
